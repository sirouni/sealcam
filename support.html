Support

SealCam (iOS) · 中文

Email sirouni@gmail.com. Include your iOS version, what you were doing, and a screenshot if it helps.

Subscriptions

Buy, restore, and cancel through Apple. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Checking a file

Free, on the phone, nothing uploads. Open a .sealcam, or an original from AirDrop / Files. A compressed chat-app image usually comes back Inconclusive — it isn’t the original anymore.

Sealing

Needs a network round-trip to Apple App Attest the first time. After that, sealing stays on device. If a required extra is on (location or trusted time) and it can’t complete, that shot fails.

支持

写信到 sirouni@gmail.com。请带上 iOS 版本、当时在做什么，有截图更好。

订阅

购买、恢复和取消都走 Apple。iPhone：设置 → 你的姓名 → 订阅。

验文件

一直免费，在手机上完成，照片不上传。请打开 .sealcam，或 AirDrop /「文件」里的原件。聊天软件压缩图通常只能显示无法鉴定——文件已经不是原件。

签发

第一次签发需要联网走一遍 Apple App Attest。之后签发在本机完成。若打开了定位或可信时间却拿不到结果，这次拍摄会失败。
