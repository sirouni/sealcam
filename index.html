SealCam

Seal when shot. Check for edits.

Capture in SealCam and write a checkable seal. Send the original file. The other person opens it in the same app and can see whether it changed after sealing.

Photos do not upload. Verification stays free. iOS only.

How it works

  1. 1.0

    Capture and seal

    Shoot a photo or video in this app. The seal is written into the file. No filters. The free allowance is 6 photos and 2 videos (each at most 60 seconds).

  2. 2.0

    Send the original

    On social networks, send a .sealcam file. AirDrop and file transfer can send the original photo or video. A compressed WeChat image usually cannot be verified — that is not a bug; the file is no longer the original.

  3. 3.0

    They open it in SealCam

    The verifier must install SealCam. There are three results: unchanged, tampered, or inconclusive. Verification runs on the device and does not go through our servers.

What it does, and does not

Does Show whether the original file changed after sealing, and whether a genuine copy of this app issued it. Photos and videos are not uploaded to our servers.
Does not It does not stop rephotography, prove the scene is real, prove the shot happened at a given second, or hold on a jailbroken device. iOS only for now.
Pro Unlimited issuance, plus lens heading, a context frame, and trusted time at issuance. Those three are for comparison only and do not prove the shot was not a recapture.
