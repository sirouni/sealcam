How it works

  1. 1.0

    Shoot

    Photo or video, no filters. Free: 6 photos and 2 videos, 60 seconds each.

  2. 2.0

    Send the original

    On chat apps, send a .sealcam file. AirDrop and Files keep the original. A compressed WeChat image usually fails — it isn’t the original anymore.

  3. 3.0

    Open it in SealCam

    They need the app too. Three results: Unchanged, Tampered, or Inconclusive. It runs on the phone.

What it covers

Does Show whether the file changed after it was sealed, and whether this app sealed it. We don’t host your photos.
Doesn’t Catch a photo of a screen. Prove the scene is real. Prove the exact shutter time. Work on a jailbroken phone. Android.
Pro Unlimited seals, plus heading, a wide-angle snapshot, and trusted time. Those extras are context. They don’t prove it wasn’t a recapture.
